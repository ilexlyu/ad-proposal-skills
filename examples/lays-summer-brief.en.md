# Example: An Ambiguous Lay's Summer Brief

[简体中文](lays-summer-brief.md)

This fictional brief is intentionally incomplete. It approximates the ambiguity teams often receive at the beginning of a real proposal process.

## Client brief

We are planning a summer campaign for Lay's aimed at younger consumers.

We want the brand to play a more visible role across travel, streaming, friend gatherings, and other summer occasions.

The campaign should feel social rather than promotional or overly commercial.

Budget is still open. Digital is the priority, with possible Xiaohongshu, Douyin, and offline extensions.

Help us define the direction first.

## Recommended skill flow

1. **`ad-proposal-orchestrator`** — classify the assignment, expose missing inputs, define assumptions, and sequence the work.
2. **`ad-research-collector`** — research the category, summer occasions, platform behavior, brand assets, and competitor activity with traceable sources.
3. **`ad-insight-reviewer`** — turn evidence into scored strategic interpretations and select the strongest direction.
4. **`ad-creative-methods`** — translate the chosen insight into creative territories through only the methods relevant to the problem.
5. **`ad-proposal-narrative`** — build the client-facing argument and slide-by-slide blueprint.
6. **`ad-ppt-visual-architect`** — define an executable visual system and design handoff.
7. **`ad-delivery-review`** — inspect logic, evidence, stakeholder risk, and reusable learning.

## Illustrative output

```text
Strategic direction
Lay's should not treat summer as another promotion or flavor launch. It should
own the small social moments that begin around a shared snack and become the
default cue for starting a stream, trip, gathering, or late-night conversation.

Creative platform
Open with a crunch.

Channel roles
Xiaohongshu establishes occasions, Douyin amplifies the repeatable action,
and offline touchpoints close the path to purchase.
```

This example demonstrates workflow output only. It is not an official Lay's campaign or brand endorsement.
